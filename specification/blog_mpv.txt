Given the scope, I would not start by building the entire open Internet publishing ecosystem. The risk is spending years building infrastructure before proving that people actually want the core loop.

I would start by building the smallest version that proves the central idea:

"Creators publish, measure, experiment, improve, and compete."

Phase 1: Build the "Creator Arena" MVP

Ignore books, federation, search engines, and plugins initially.

Build:

1. A blogging platform

Users can:

Create a profile
Publish posts
Follow creators
Read posts
Comment
Like/save

Basic but functional.

2. A serious analytics dashboard

This is your differentiator.

For every post:

The dashboard should feel like a game.

Your first milestone

Don't aim for "build the future of publishing."

Aim for:

100 writers publish 10 posts each, run experiments, and check their dashboards every week.

If people become obsessed with improving their metrics, you have the foundation.

The hardest part is not the technology. The hardest part is creating the loop that makes a writer think:

"I want to publish one more article because I know I can improve my score."

Once that exists, the books, search, open ecosystem, and developer contributions become natural extensions.



That feature actually fits extremely well. It creates a content lifecycle: a blog post is not the final product; it is a seed that can grow into a larger intellectual asset.

The platform could treat articles → collections → books as a natural progression.
